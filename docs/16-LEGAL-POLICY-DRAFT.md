# Sharma Video Care — Legal & Policy Draft

## Important
This is a product/legal-content draft for implementation and review, not legal advice. Before public launch, the business should have the final Terms, Privacy Policy, warranty/returns policy and any required business disclosures reviewed for the actual Sharma Video Care entity and operations.

Nepal has an Electronic Commerce Act, 2081, a Consumer Protection Act, 2075, and a Privacy Act, 2075. These should be checked against the final operating model before launch. ## A. Terms of Service — product structure
The final Terms should cover:

### 1. About Sharma Video Care
- Legal/business name.
- Registered/business address.
- Contact details.
- Website/app ownership.
- Effective date and update process.

### 2. Services
- Repair and maintenance.
- Inspection.
- Custom service requests.
- Installation/setup.
- Technician visits.
- Service scheduling.

### 3. Inspection and quotation
Recommended customer-facing rule:
> Inspection and initial visit are currently free. Where diagnosis is required, the final repair quotation is provided after inspection. No repair should begin beyond the approved scope without customer approval, except where an explicitly agreed emergency/authorization rule applies.

### 4. Product purchases
Cover:
- Product descriptions.
- Prices.
- Availability.
- Order acceptance.
- Delivery.
- COD.
- Bank-transfer verification.
- Courier delivery.
- Cancellation.

### 5. Sourced products
Explain that customers can request products not currently listed/stocked. Sourcing availability, price and delivery timing may depend on supplier availability. No customer sourcing deposit is currently required.

### 6. Second-hand products
Explain:
- Only Sharma Video Care sells/list used products.
- Condition is disclosed per listing.
- Testing information is provided where available.
- Warranty/returns are product-specific.
- Any defects or limitations disclosed on the listing form part of the purchase information.

### 7. Warranty
Warranty must be shown per product/service. The Terms should not promise a universal warranty.

### 8. Returns/refunds
Returns/refunds must follow the applicable product/service policy and applicable law. Product-level restrictions must be disclosed before purchase.

### 9. Technician services
- Customer access/scheduling.
- Service area.
- Technician assignment.
- External technician involvement.
- Customer cooperation requirements.
- Access/safety requirements.
- Rework/warranty rules.

### 10. User conduct
Prohibit:
- Fraudulent orders.
- Abusive chat.
- False information.
- Unauthorized access.
- Malicious uploads.
- Payment fraud.
- Attempts to manipulate reviews.

### 11. Intellectual property
Cover Sharma Video Care branding, site/app content and customer-submitted content permissions needed to provide the service.

### 12. Liability
The final clause should be drafted by counsel so it does not unlawfully exclude responsibilities that cannot be excluded.

### 13. Changes
Explain how Terms may be updated and when changes take effect.

### 14. Contact/dispute process
Provide official contact information and a customer support/escalation process.

## B. Privacy Policy — product structure
The Privacy Policy should explain:
- What data is collected: name, phone, email, addresses, orders, service requests, uploaded media, chat messages, payment proof, device/app information as applicable.
- Why data is collected.
- How it is used to provide services, process orders, communicate and prevent fraud.
- Who receives it: authorized Sharma Video Care staff, assigned technicians, courier partners, communication providers and necessary service providers.
- Storage/security practices.
- Retention periods.
- Customer rights/requests as applicable.
- Contact for privacy requests.
- Cookies/analytics on the website.
- Third-party services.
- Children/minors policy.
- Data breach/security incident process.
- Policy update process.

Do not collect more data than needed for the relevant service.

## C. Warranty & Returns framework
Implement policies as configurable records rather than hard-coded global rules.

Example fields:
- policyId
- scopeType: PRODUCT | CATEGORY | SERVICE | USED_PRODUCT
- duration
- coverage
- exclusions
- returnEligible
- returnWindow
- refundMethod
- replacementEligible
- notes
- effectiveFrom
- effectiveTo

The customer must see the applicable policy before checkout or service approval.

## D. Recommended legal UX
At checkout/service approval:
- Show a concise summary of applicable warranty/return terms.
- Link to full policy.
- Require explicit acknowledgement where appropriate.
- Store the policy version accepted by the customer.

## E. Business disclosures
Admin should be able to maintain:
- Business name.
- Registration/tax details where applicable.
- Address.
- Phone/email.
- Support hours.
- Service area.
- Delivery policy.
- Payment instructions.
